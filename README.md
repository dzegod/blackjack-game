## Introduction

**WHAT IS IT**

Blackjack is very popular game with cards around the world. Also it is very addictive because it does not require very high knowledge to understand how to play it, but you can count cards and understand how it works. Thats the reason why casinos does have this game - it plays tricks with people who came to play to keep business going. Counting cards as customer is not illegal but it could lead you to a big troubles.

That is opposite how my created program called - **Blackjack-game** works. The rules are the same, but you can not count cards there, because program generates for you random cards. You dont come here to win money, you come here to try your luck and have a good time.

---

**MAIN MENU**

 When you start program there will be a few options, controlled by numbers: 1- **continue** - it saves your progress [nickname, coins] ; 2 - **Register** if you are new member. There you will be asked for your nickname and in the start you will get 500 coins to play; and of course, 3 - **Close** which closes the program. Maximum users you can have is 3. When you reach your maximum users, it will say "Maximum number of users reached"
